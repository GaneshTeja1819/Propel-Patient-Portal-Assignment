# User Story - US_017

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_017

## Story Title
* Automated Preferred Slot Swap Background Job

## Description
* As a Patient who registered a preferred slot, I want the system to automatically move my appointment to my preferred time when it becomes available, so that I get my ideal slot without having to check availability or take any manual action.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Swap job triggers when a slot is released
- **Given**: A WaitlistEntry exists for a slot that has just been released by another patient's cancellation or reschedule
- **When**: The slot release event occurs
- **Then**: The Hangfire slot-swap job is triggered within 5 seconds of the slot release; the job evaluates all WaitlistEntry records for that slot ordered by registeredAt timestamp (UC-008)

### AC-002: First eligible patient is atomically moved to the preferred slot
- **Given**: One or more WaitlistEntry records exist for the released slot
- **When**: The Hangfire job selects the first eligible entry (earliest registeredAt)
- **Then**: The patient's Appointment is moved from their currently booked slot to the preferred slot in a single atomic database transaction; the previously booked slot is released to the available pool simultaneously; the WaitlistEntry is deleted (UC-008)

### AC-003: Email notification confirms the swap to the patient
- **Given**: The atomic slot swap has completed successfully
- **When**: The Hangfire job reaches the notification step
- **Then**: An email is dispatched to the patient's registered email address confirming the swap with the new appointment date and time; the Notification record is created with status "Sent" or "Failed" (FR-013, FR-019, UC-008)

### AC-004: Atomic swap failure triggers one retry then skips entry
- **Given**: The atomic swap transaction fails due to a concurrency conflict
- **When**: Hangfire processes the failure
- **Then**: The job retries the swap once; if the second attempt also fails, the WaitlistEntry is skipped and the next eligible entry is evaluated; the failure is logged in the audit log with the patientId, slotId, and error details (UC-008 Extension 4a)

### AC-005: No eligible waitlist entries results in no action
- **Given**: A slot is released but no WaitlistEntry exists for it
- **When**: The Hangfire job evaluates the waitlist
- **Then**: The job completes with no state changes; the slot becomes available in the general pool; no notifications are sent (UC-008 Extension 3a)

## Edge Cases
* Email delivery fails after a successful swap → the swap remains in effect; the Notification record is marked "Failed"; the audit log records the delivery failure; the patient's appointment IS on the new slot despite no notification being received
* Multiple concurrent releases for the same slot → Hangfire's job queue ensures only one slot-swap job evaluates a given slot at a time using a distributed lock or queue key; no double-swap occurs
* Hangfire job is processed after the preferred slot has been rebooked by another patient → the swap transaction detects the rowVersion conflict; the swap is abandoned; the patient's original appointment is unchanged; the WaitlistEntry is retained for future opportunities
