# User Story - US_020

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_020

## Story Title
* Automated Appointment Reminders via Email and SMS

## Description
* As a Patient with a booked appointment, I want to receive automated reminders via email and SMS at configurable intervals before my appointment, so that I don't forget my appointment and the clinic's no-show rate is reduced.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Reminders are dispatched at configured pre-appointment intervals
- **Given**: A future Appointment record exists with a patient's registered email and phone number
- **When**: The Hangfire reminder job triggers at the configured interval before the appointment date (e.g., 24 hours and 2 hours before)
- **Then**: An email reminder and an SMS reminder are dispatched to the patient's registered contact details; the Notification records for both channels are created with status "Queued" transitioning to "Sent" (FR-018, UC-012)

### AC-002: Email is sent via configured SMTP gateway
- **Given**: The SMTP provider (Brevo or equivalent free-tier) is configured via environment variable
- **When**: The email reminder job executes
- **Then**: The email is delivered to the patient's inbox with the appointment date, time, and a recognisable sender; delivery status is recorded in the Notification record (TR-010)

### AC-003: SMS is sent via configured free-tier SMS gateway
- **Given**: The SMS gateway credentials are configured via environment variable and the patient has a registered phone number
- **When**: The SMS reminder job executes
- **Then**: An SMS with the appointment date and time is delivered; delivery status is recorded; if the patient has no phone number registered, the SMS step is skipped without error

### AC-004: Delivery failures are retried and logged
- **Given**: An email or SMS gateway is unavailable when a reminder dispatch is attempted
- **When**: The Hangfire job encounters a delivery error
- **Then**: The job retries up to 3 times with exponential back-off (10 s, 60 s, 360 s); after all retries fail, the Notification record status is set to "Failed"; the appointment is not affected; the failure is logged (UC-012 Extension 4a)

### AC-005: Cancelled appointments do not receive reminders
- **Given**: A patient has a cancelled Appointment
- **When**: The reminder job evaluates the appointment for dispatch
- **Then**: No reminder is sent for cancelled appointments; the job skips the record without error

## Edge Cases
* Patient's phone number is in an unsupported international format → the SMS dispatch returns a gateway error; the failure is logged; the email reminder is still sent; no user-facing error appears
* Hangfire job fires at the same time as the appointment (zero lead time) → the job evaluates the appointment start time; if the appointment is within 1 minute, the reminder is skipped as it would arrive after the appointment
* Appointment is rescheduled after reminders have already been queued → the old reminder Hangfire jobs are cancelled when the reschedule completes; new reminder jobs are queued for the new appointment date and time
